Frequently asked questions

Social media questions, answered.

What kind of social media content do you create?

We create short video, before-and-after clips, menu and event photography, scroll-stopping posts, and QR codes that link to your menu, booking page or Google review form.

Do you film on location?

Yes. We shoot at your business, whether that is a kitchen, a jobsite, a showroom or a storefront, so the content shows your real work.

Can I post the content myself?

Yes. The Elevate package can be fully managed by us, or built for you to run on your own. We hand over the content, captions and a posting calendar either way.

How often should a business post?

Consistency matters more than volume. We recommend a steady weekly rhythm that you can keep up, and we set it up around your time and goals.

Does social media content help with Google and AI search?

Social content does not replace local SEO, but it builds the reviews, mentions and recent activity that make your business look active and trustworthy to both people and search tools.
