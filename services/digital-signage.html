Frequently asked questions

Digital signage questions, answered.

What is a digital menu board?

A digital menu board is a screen that shows your menu, prices, specials and new items. You can update it instantly, so you never need to reprint a menu again.

Can I sell ad space on my screens?

Yes. Many venues rent a rotating slot to other local businesses, which creates a second revenue stream. We set up the ad placement options and the rate structure, and you choose which businesses you want to feature.

Which businesses are a good fit?

Restaurants, bakeries, coffee shops, bars and food trucks get the most from digital menu boards and signage, because customers see the screen while deciding what to order.

Do I need special equipment?

We recommend the screen and player setup based on your space and budget. We'll explain the options on the discovery call so there are no surprises.

Can you design the menu and ads too?

Yes. We design the menu board, event showcases and ad creative, and we can pair the signage with social media content and a Google Business Profile so your brand stays consistent everywhere.
