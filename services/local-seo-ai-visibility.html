Frequently asked questions

Local SEO and AI visibility, answered.

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is how often, and how accurately, AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude mention your business when customers ask for a recommendation in your area or industry.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making your business easy for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite. Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in search results. GEO and AEO (answer engine optimization) focus on being named and cited in AI-generated answers. The two work together.

Do you guarantee a first-page Google ranking?

No one can guarantee a ranking, because Google and AI tools change their systems and results. We show you the measurable steps we take, track the results, and tell you honestly what is working.

How long does local SEO take?

Most local businesses see early movement within a few months of consistent work on their Google Business Profile, reviews, website content and local listings. Results vary by market and starting point.
