Frequently asked questions

Startup kit questions, answered.

What is included in a startup kit?

A startup kit covers the basics to open your doors: business formation (LLC, corporation, sole proprietorship or DBA), an EIN and tax ID, a logo, business cards, invoice and quote templates, a website, NFC tap cards and Google review tap cards.

Do I have to form an LLC?

No. We help you choose the structure that fits your business, whether that is an LLC, a corporation, a sole proprietorship or a DBA. For tax and legal advice specific to your situation, talk to a licensed attorney or CPA.

Can you help with the EIN and tax ID?

Yes. We walk you through getting your federal EIN and the state tax registrations your business needs, and we make sure the details match across your formation documents, invoices and website.

What are NFC tap cards and Google review cards?

An NFC tap card lets a customer tap their phone to your card and instantly open your contact info, social profiles, Google Business Profile, booking link or payment link. A Google review card does the same and opens straight to your Google review form.

Do you build the website too?

Yes. Startup kits include a clean, mobile-friendly website built to be found on Google, with the basic local SEO set up from day one.

How long does a startup kit take?

Timing depends on the formation and filing steps set by state and federal agencies. Once your details are ready, branding and templates typically take a few weeks. We'll give you a timeline on the discovery call.
